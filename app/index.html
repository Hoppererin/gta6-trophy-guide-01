    Roadmap to the Platinum

    Built from the official trophy descriptions: play the story first, then the side characters, then explore, race and rob — and save the 100% cleanup for last. Each stage lists the trophies it covers, in the order to earn them.

    Missable trophies

    Only one trophy in the whole list is flagged as possibly missable by the guides. Everything else either unlocks by playing the story or stays available in free roam after the finale.

    Why the rest cannot be missed

      Save habits that cost nothing

      • Keep a manual save from before any late-story mission, in case a spot or a character becomes unavailable.
      • Keep cheats off your main save: using cheats disables trophies and saving, according to GTABase and TechWiser.
      • Do the meditation spots as you explore — do not leave Positive Mental Attitude for after the finale.
